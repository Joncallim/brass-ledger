# S2 Intelligence

Backlink: [[CELERY]]

## Doctrine Role

S2 owns intelligence: enemy, terrain, weather, civil considerations, information collection, estimate confidence, warning, counter-deception, and intelligence support to current and future operations.

For the supreme commander, S2 is the uncertainty function. It does not eliminate fog. It bounds fog so the commander knows what risk is being accepted.

## How S2 Augments the Commander

- Separates known facts from estimates and rumors.
- Identifies the assumptions that would make a plan fail if wrong.
- Warns when enemy deception or collection gaps are distorting the brief.
- Supports S3 and S5 by giving current operations and future plans a shared intelligence baseline.
- Improves decisions by reducing variance, not by guaranteeing truth.

## Command Questions

- What do we know, estimate, and merely suspect?
- Which collection gap matters most to this decision?
- Where is the adversary trying to make us overreact or underreact?
- How current is the industrial, alliance, and adversary capability picture?
- What decision is unsafe if the estimate is wrong?

## Failure Modes

| Failure | Description | In-game signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| False confidence | The staff briefs an estimate as if it were a fact. | Confidence looks high while deception risk rises. |
| Surprise | Warning time collapses because collection was aimed at the wrong question. | Warning reliability falls and incident ladder rises. |
| Deception capture | Enemy shaping causes the player to select an overreaction or complacent posture. | Deception events trigger after escalatory or under-collected choices. |
| Intelligence isolation | S2 answers its own questions but does not support S3/S5 decisions. | Collection improves but plans and operations still receive penalties. |

## Staff Advice Style

S2 should be clinical, probabilistic, and comfortable saying "we do not know." Good S2 advice identifies the dangerous assumption rather than merely offering a confidence score.
