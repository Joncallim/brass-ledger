# Northern Frontier

Backlink: [[../world-overview]]

## Role

The Northern Frontier is the recommended first campaign theater. It is small enough for a 12-turn campaign but dense enough to exercise the full S1-S5 model: maritime posture, reserve strain, intelligence ambiguity, lift and fuel constraints, partner reassurance, domestic cover, and escalation management.

## Geography

The theater contains:

- The Kestrel Run sea lane.
- The Varro Islands, a Sanctum League island chain with fuel and medical vulnerabilities.
- The Larkspur Shelf, shallow water with gas infrastructure and undersea cables.
- The Northgate rail corridor, which connects inland river markets to automated ports.
- The Helix-operated Cascabel Terminal.
- Veyr coastal exclusion zones backed by drones, patrol craft, and missile batteries.

## Military Problem

The theater rewards credible posture but punishes spectacle. Forces can move quickly on maps and briefing slides, but real endurance depends on fuel contracts, depot flow, training seats, reserve predictability, and local consent.

## Campaign Questions

- Can the player restore warning reliability after Kestrel Run without chasing every signal?
- Can visible deterrence remain executable and supportable?
- Can partners be reassured without raising domestic media heat past cabinet tolerance?
- Can sustainment investments arrive before repeated tempo creates culmination?
- Can the player keep escalation below open general war while still preventing fait accompli moves?

## Key Constraints

| Constraint | Gameplay meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Commercial shipping market | Determines lift availability and convoy feasibility. |
| Trusted electronics chain | Limits sensors, C2 upgrades, depot tooling, and precision fires integration. |
| Propellant market | Limits fires-heavy deterrence and munitions hedging. |
| Reserve employers | Convert repeated call-ups into domestic and S1 pressure. |
| Partner parliament schedules | Make alliance alignment time-sensitive. |
| Cable attribution uncertainty | Raises S2 deception risk and S5 narrative pressure. |

## Scenario Seeds

- "Distant overwatch": The player starts with low escalation but weak partner confidence.
- "Forward reassurance": The player starts with higher visible posture but worse reserve strain and lift burn.
- "Cabinet restraint": The player starts with strong domestic cover but lower alliance reassurance.
- "Bad brief": The player starts with higher confidence and higher deception risk, creating an attractive trap.
