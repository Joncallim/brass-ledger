## Worldbuilding Rule

The setting should feel like historical fiction with modern technology, not fantasy reskinned as geopolitics. Factions may echo real historical pressures such as alliance fatigue, industrial dependency, civil-military integration, professional-force exhaustion, commercial capture, occupation memory, sanctions, and proxy competition, but they must not copy real states directly.

## Playable Promise

Every faction must create a distinct commander problem:

- What can this faction do better than others?
- What breaks first when it overuses that advantage?
- Which S1-S5 staff function sees the failure early?
- Which political story keeps the faction fighting after the first shock?
