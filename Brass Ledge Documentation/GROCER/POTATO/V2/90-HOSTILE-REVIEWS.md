# Current structural invariants

Implementation/lab must try to falsify:

1. adversary reads only authorised observations;
2. #100/#102 raw hidden facts are confined to named observation extractors; evidence mappers/reducers never see posture/arbitrary world state;
3. relied-upon #99 paths normal-play reachable;
4. all-Delegate staff package legal;
5. exactly three commander-only courses cost one intervention and never delegate;
6. standing direction materially changes advice/consequence;
7. **assessment, warning and public-case basis are demonstrably separate**;
8. routine C3 observation is bounded/non-omniscient and action-specific situation prose cannot become analysis;
9. historical intelligence uses historical entries, never current state;
10. Lattice/reroute/liaison are mechanically distinct and no target globally dominates all co-eligible states;
11. C5 consultation-vs-tempo and attribution-use-vs-preserve elastic;
12. C5 Operations responds to warning only after higher command filters;
13. C5 state/signals issue-order independent and non-contradictory;
14. no dead state/observation kept for symmetry;
15. recovery real/costly through C5;
16. no player-safe course accidental dominated trap, intervention opportunity included;
17. every terminal state has a response and no displayed final pair dominated;
18. terminal/source/commitment costs authoritative/disclosed;
19. pure #100 semantics are protected by content identity without persisted format bump;
20. V1/replay/version/safe-projection boundaries isolated;
21. machine evidence never substitutes for human fun.

# Remaining human-owned unknowns

No design review can honestly settle:

- whether two interventions feels right;
- whether six windows is right pacing;
- whether players understand/own implicit delegation;
- whether silence/routine political baselines feel disciplined or passive;
- whether the difference between “assessment” and “warning” is understandable without jargon;
- whether the Intelligence Chief brief is concise/useful rather than opaque;
- whether Lattice target questions feel genuinely strategic rather than technical;
- whether public-case/source trade is legible;
- whether prose density/tone is enjoyable;
- whether consequences create regret/vindication/tension/surprise;
- whether real players voluntarily continue/replay.

Those remain `#107 → formative smoke → browser → formal human gate`.

# Review stop rule

Do not keep changing a mechanically coherent uncertain choice because another balance can be imagined.

Once contracts are internally consistent, replay/content-identity safe, non-omniscient, free of known dominated/dead/cosmetic mechanics, recovery-capable and protected by #107 diagnostics, further balance changes require implemented reachable-state lab results or human evidence.
