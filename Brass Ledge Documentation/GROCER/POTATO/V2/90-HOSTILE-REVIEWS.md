# V2 Hostile Reviews

Backlink: [[README]]

This records material product/architecture defects found by repeated adversarial review. It is intentionally not a victory log: each pass attempted to falsify the composed game, not merely verify previous prose.

A review can validate structure. It cannot certify human fun.

## Current structural invariants to attack during implementation

Every domain review/lab should actively try to falsify:

1. normal adversary policy reads only authorised observations;
2. collection result never reads hidden posture;
3. every claimed #99 behavior transition is reachable through normal authored signals where product relies on it;
4. all-Delegate staff intent is always a legal complete command set;
5. commander-only liaison always consumes one intervention;
6. standing direction has real causal recommendation/consequence impact;
7. C2 reroute monitoring, Lattice targets and liaison are mechanically distinct;
8. C5 consultation-vs-tempo and attribution-use-vs-preserve choices remain elastic;
9. C5 state/signal results independent of issue/array order;
10. no legal package creates contradictory same-signal values;
11. visible vs quiet reinforcement have different adversary consequences;
12. recovery remains real/costly through C5;
13. no player-safe course is an unintentional dominated trap;
14. no final route is universal and player-safe dominated terminal routes are pruned;
15. terminal route/source/commitment costs appear in authoritative post-route state/classification and are disclosed when knowable;
16. V1/replay/version/player-safe projection boundaries remain isolated;
17. machine evidence never substitutes for human fun evidence.

## Remaining human-owned unknowns

No further design review can settle honestly:

- whether two interventions feels right;
- whether six windows is right pacing;
- whether players understand/own implicit delegation;
- whether Lattice feels satisfying rather than merely useful;
- whether intelligence feels intriguing rather than opaque;
- whether consultation/tempo, source exposure and attribution timing are understandable without over-explanation;
- whether prose density/tone is enjoyable;
- whether consequences create actual regret/vindication/tension/surprise;
- whether real players voluntarily continue/replay.

Those remain the `#107 → formative smoke → browser → formal human gate` programme.

## Review stop rule

Do not keep changing a mechanically coherent uncertain choice merely because an agent can imagine another balance.

Once contracts are:

- internally consistent;
- replay-safe;
- non-omniscient;
- free of known player-safe dominated traps;
- recovery-capable;
- guarded by #107 elasticity/dominance/fairness diagnostics;

further balance changes require actual reachable-state laboratory results or human evidence.
