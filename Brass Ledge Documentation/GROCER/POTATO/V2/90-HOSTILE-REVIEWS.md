# Composed-system pass 7 — exceptional authority / free political baselines

The final non-terminal command audit asked whether an action could become a **free delegated improvement** or an exceptional irreversible act merely because standing metadata happened to match.

| Defect | Amendment |
| --- | --- |
| C2 Political staff baseline was joint warning, making silence a mostly pointless intervention and giving partner benefit for free under every philosophy. | Staff baseline changed to `remain-silent`; partner priority/style can still delegate joint warning, otherwise commander spends attention for the benefit. |
| C3 Political staff baseline was reassurance whenever issue existed, again making the weaker routine option pointless and political repair free. | Staff baseline changed to `routine-contact`; partner priority/style delegates reassurance, otherwise it is a personal intervention. |
| Visible-deterrence standing style could make unsupported C2 public accusation the staff recommendation. | `public-accusation` is now commander-only (`requiresIntervention = true`). |
| Partner-oriented standing direction could make C5 public attribution use the staff recommendation and silently burn the protected source. | `use-attribution` is now commander-only; staff always Holds when issue exists. |
| Liaison was already commander-only but the authority concept was documented as if unique. | Final exact set is **three**: C2 accusation, C4 liaison, C5 attribution use. No other Kestrel course has the flag. |
| Local dominance could falsely conclude a directly beneficial intervention dominates a routine delegated baseline while ignoring the scarce token and foreclosed other intervention. | Local dominance now compares **complete legal packages including intervention tokens consumed, remaining budget and same-cycle action-space opportunity cost**. |
| Presentation could infer exceptional authority from labels or only know about liaison. | `38` projects explicit safe `requiresIntervention` semantics; `28/34` prove exact-three behavior. |

This pass is important for the product thesis: headquarters should act autonomously under standing direction, but the player must still personally own the exceptional acts that spend unsupported public attribution, emergency political access or a protected intelligence source.

# Current structural invariants

Implementation/lab must try to falsify:

1. adversary reads only authorised observations;
2. collection never reads hidden posture;
3. relied-upon #99 paths normal-play reachable;
4. all-Delegate staff package legal;
5. **exactly C2 accusation, C4 liaison and C5 attribution use are commander-only; each costs one intervention and never delegates**;
6. standing direction materially changes advice/consequence, including political benefit baselines;
7. reroute/Lattice targets/liaison mechanically distinct;
8. C5 consultation-vs-tempo and attribution-use-vs-preserve elastic;
9. C5 state/signals issue-order independent;
10. no contradictory same-signal values;
11. visible/quiet reinforcement different adversary consequences;
12. no dead state/observation kept for symmetry;
13. recovery real/costly through C5;
14. no player-safe course accidental dominated trap, with intervention opportunity cost included;
15. every terminal state has response and no displayed final pair dominated;
16. terminal/source/commitment costs authoritative and disclosed when knowable;
17. V1/replay/version/safe-projection boundaries isolated;
18. machine evidence never substitutes for human fun.

# Remaining human-owned unknowns

No further design review can honestly settle:

- whether two interventions feels right;
- whether six windows is right pacing;
- whether players understand/own implicit delegation;
- whether political silence/routine baselines feel like disciplined HQ behavior or implausible passivity under non-partner strategies;
- whether Lattice feels satisfying rather than merely useful;
- whether intelligence feels intriguing rather than opaque;
- whether consultation/tempo, source exposure and attribution timing are understandable without over-explanation;
- whether prose density/tone is enjoyable;
- whether consequences create actual regret/vindication/tension/surprise;
- whether real players voluntarily continue/replay.

Those remain `#107 → formative smoke → browser → formal human gate`.

# Review stop rule

Do not keep changing a mechanically coherent uncertain choice merely because another balance can be imagined.

Once contracts are internally consistent, replay-safe, non-omniscient, free of known player-safe dominated traps/dead state/automatic exceptional authority, recovery-capable and protected by #107 diagnostics, further balance changes require implemented reachable-state lab results or human evidence.
