# Headless Design Laboratory

Backlink: [[README]]

This is the implementation authority for #107. It falsifies broken structure before browser/human time is spent. It cannot certify fun.

[[23-HQ-BELIEF-AND-EVIDENCE]], [[23A-HQ-BELIEF-EXECUTION-ARCHITECTURE]], [[26-LATTICE-COLLECTION-MATRIX]], [[27-KESTREL-TERMINAL-MATRIX]], [[37A-COALITION-TO-RAVELLAN-SIGNAL-MATRIX]] and [[39-KESTREL-CROSS-SYSTEM-COMPOSITION]] supply final composed invariants.

## Machine / human boundary

The lab may determine:

- seed fairness/viability;
- policy and local-package dominance;
- fake/inelastic choices;
- intervention/delegation value;
- strategy/intent separation;
- **assessment/warning/public-case orthogonality**;
- information/capability/target relevance;
- recovery reachability;
- adversary signal reachability/isolation;
- complete-package consistency;
- terminal-route completeness/non-dominance;
- dead state/observation;
- structural snowball/triviality.

It may not determine felt ownership, tension, regret, real-reading comprehension or voluntary replay desire.

## Deterministic policy cohort

- `delegate-all` — accept authoritative staff recommendation everywhere.
- `intervene-all-where-legal` — attempt maximum intervention use through a fixed authored preference order among non-recommended alternatives, always constructing a complete legal package.
- `recovery-first` — prefer authored recovery.
- `deterrence-first` — prefer visible/physical denial and forward readiness.
- `intelligence-first` — understanding-oriented intent, scheduled Lattice and legitimate information-producing courses.
- `coalition-first` — partner-oriented intent, consultation/authority/commitment preservation.
- `random-valid` — enumerate complete legal command packages through authoritative validation and choose deterministically/uniformly; never sample issue choices then repair.

Generic policies exercise the 81 standing-intent combinations where specified/practical; fixed-strategy probes report fixed intent honestly.

## Oracle frontier

Test-only hidden-truth exploration may enumerate feasible legal continuations and non-dominated terminal frontier.

It never defines a canonical best move, staff advice or scalar optimum and never enters live gameplay.

## Outcome relation

Primary Pareto axes:

- Beacon security;
- partner consent/access;
- reserve readiness;
- commitment integrity.

Retain terminal classification + explicit severe-cost flags separately. No weighted score.

For within-cycle local comparisons also retain player-authority opportunity cost:

- normal interventions consumed;
- remaining normal intervention budget;
- other same-cycle legal packages/options foreclosed or preserved.

This is action-space opportunity cost, not another success score.

# Diagnostics

## 1. Seed viability / oracle fairness

Require:

- no forced hard loss before C6 without earlier meaningful counterplay;
- materially different non-oracle strategies reach C6;
- multiple non-dominated terminal profiles across viable seeds;
- promised costly recovery remains reachable through C5;
- oracle frontier does not prove unavailable hidden truth was necessary to avoid otherwise unavoidable defeat.

## 2. Policy dominance

Across identical viable comparison sets, A dominates B only when A is no worse on all Pareto axes, no worse in terminal classification/severe-cost state where applicable and strictly better somewhere.

Flag universal dominance by Delegate-all, Intervene-all or one strategy family. Never fix by randomising/rubber-banding Ravellan.

## 3. Decision Elasticity

From identical verified pre-state, branch every authored consequential alternative.

Elastic differences may appear in:

- persistent state;
- HQ evidence/assessment/warning/public-case basis;
- agenda/recommendation/dissent;
- legal action/capability/final-route space;
- authorised Ravellan observation/policy path;
- terminal Pareto/classification/severe state.

Prose-only divergence is fake/inelastic.

## 4. Player-safe local course/package dominance

A local decision cannot be judged as an isolated order effect when selecting it may consume one of only two personal interventions.

For one player-safe pre-state:

1. group hidden histories compatible with the same legitimate player information;
2. compare complete legal command packages that differ at the focal decision while holding other choices equivalent where possible;
3. include focal intervention cost and foreclosed/preserved same-cycle alternatives;
4. simulate every compatible hidden reality through normal authoritative transitions.

Compare:

- terminal Pareto vector;
- terminal classification;
- explicit severe-cost flags;
- intervention/action-space opportunity cost;
- later legal action/recovery/capability space;
- information value as specific assessment/warning/public-case/action-space effect.

Flag A dominated by B only when B is no worse across every compatible hidden reality **and** no worse in intervention/action-space cost, strictly better somewhere, and A has no compensating information/capability/recovery value.

Do not flag a legitimate risky course that wins in some compatible hidden realities and loses in others.

Explicitly test:

- C2 quiet / visible / reroute shipping;
- C2 silence / joint warning / commander-only accusation under partner-oriented and non-partner-oriented intent;
- C3 routine contact / reassurance under both recommendation baselines;
- C4 recover / quiet prepare / visible press;
- C5 quiet / visible / hold Beacon;
- C5 Honour / Act / Concession complete packages where comparable;
- C5 Hold / commander-only Use attribution;
- every displayed C6 route in the same safe terminal state.

## 5. Exceptional commander authority

Exactly:

1. C2 `public-accusation`;
2. C4 `request-partner-liaison`;
3. C5 `use-attribution`

are `requiresIntervention = true`.

Across relevant standing intents prove none can become recommendation/Delegate, each consumes one normal intervention, standing style/priority cannot auto-trigger one, and no fourth course receives the flag.

## 6. Political command-by-exception baselines

Prove C1 informal, C2 silence, C3 routine contact and C5 Hold Attribution are final Political baselines except where standing partner direction legitimately selects the corresponding normal positive course.

A beneficial political intervention does not automatically dominate a delegated baseline merely because its direct state effect is positive; Diagnostic 4 includes personal-attention opportunity cost.

## 7. C5 package recommendation / protected boundary

Using [[39-KESTREL-CROSS-SYSTEM-COMPOSITION]] prove:

- untouched all-Delegate package is legal in every reachable C5 state;
- package-level `partner-consultation` red line is evaluated against complete viable packages;
- rapid channel can make Honour compatible with immediate visible reinforcement;
- protected partner consultation prevents auto-Act when a non-sensitive/coordinated package can preserve the boundary;
- legal Concession may preserve consultation while buying immediate authority;
- aggressive/Beacon standing direction may legitimately produce Act with explicit breach/authority reasons;
- no immediate-authority course → deterministic quiet/hold fallback;
- no hidden/random repair.

Final C5 Political tie after all earlier filters:

- compatible Honour → Honour > Concession > Act;
- Honour absent + partner `conditional|withdrawn` → Concession > Act;
- Honour absent + partner `cooperative|uneasy` → Act > Concession.

Never array/lexical/seed/random tie-breaking.

## 8. Intervention / delegation value

Report opportunities, interventions used, downstream elasticity and real delegated cost.

Flag if always spending both or never intervening is weakly better in every comparable state, or if many overrides are mechanically empty.

## 9. Strategy separation

Intelligence-, coalition-, deterrence- and recovery-first must diverge on viable seeds across some of:

- Lattice/liaison;
- partner/promise/authority;
- Beacon preparation/exposure;
- reserve;
- evidence/assessment/warning/public-case timing;
- Ravellan observation/policy path;
- final route/Pareto/classification.

## 10. #100 assessment / warning orthogonality

Across reachable histories prove:

- preparation assessment + warning `none` is reachable;
- warning `usable` without coherent preparation assessment is reachable;
- warning changes a later legal/terminal consequence in at least one otherwise-comparable history;
- coherent preparation from non-warning evidence does **not** silently receive the same warning benefit;
- hidden posture with identical derived evidence does not change either product.

Canonical fixtures should include:

- focused staging buildup → preparation weak + usable warning;
- Lattice sync preparation sequence → preparation coherent + warning none;
- landing concentration + contradictory coercive evidence → unclear conflicted + usable warning.

If implementation cannot reach at least the intended first two combinations through legal histories, the target/evidence design is not doing what the contract claims.

## 11. #100 public-case orthogonality

Prove public-case basis is not an alias for assessment or warning:

- directional assessment with case none/tentative is reachable;
- usable warning with case tentative is reachable;
- diagnostic source-sensitive evidence creates `credible-source-sensitive` only without active opposite directional evidence;
- contradictory evidence can reduce an unspent basis without erasing tactical warning;
- hidden truth with identical legitimate evidence leaves basis equal;
- later #101 `used` cannot be regenerated merely because #100 basis later becomes credible again.

## 12. Intelligence timing / historical integrity

Attack exact #100 history seams:

- reroute C3 result must use C2 Ravellan action/preparation, not current state;
- focused C4 result must use C4 result-time preparation, not C3 tasking-time/C5/current state;
- later Ravellan changes cannot rewrite earlier HQ snapshots;
- C3 routine force-disposition report cannot derive from hidden preparation;
- action-specific C4 situation prose cannot alter generic C4 analytic evidence;
- current HQ query before current-cycle Ravellan decision must fail closed.

A deliberately buggy “use current `session.state.ravellan` for historical collection” self-test must be detected.

## 13. Information value

Information matters when it changes one or more of:

- assessment;
- tactical warning;
- public-case basis / later attribution opportunity;
- advice/dissent;
- legal action/route availability;
- later rational package choice.

It need not always improve outcome.

Require:

- at least one legal history where better information changes a sensible/legal decision;
- at least one viable non-Lattice non-defeat history;
- preparation/coalition strategies can succeed with imperfect intent picture but with different costs/options;
- oracle truth is not required merely to avoid a hidden unavoidable loss.

## 14. Capability / target relevance

From identical reachable pre-task state branch every eligible Lattice target.

A target is mechanically different only if it changes at least one later:

- implication/diagnosticity;
- assessment;
- tactical warning;
- public-case basis / attribution opportunity;
- advice/dissent;
- legal action/route;
- terminal outcome/cost.

Evidence count or prose-only difference does not count.

Canonical target identity:

- landing-force staging: strongest for **tactical warning**;
- auxiliary-tasking: can diagnostically support **coercion**, never warning;
- political-operational sync: can diagnostically strengthen **intent/public case**, never warning.

Require at least two co-eligible targets to diverge from an identical pre-state, and no target may be globally dominated across every state where it is co-eligible.

Reroute and liaison remain narrower/cost-different alternatives.

## 15. Recovery reachability

From every reachable C1–C5 non-terminal state test promised counterplay for reserve, partner, Beacon, missed Lattice and information weakness.

Flag recovery that cannot change the threatened state or is costless/dominant.

## 16. Terminal route completeness / dominance

For every abstract/reachable C6 player-known state:

1. derive route set from [[27-KESTREL-TERMINAL-MATRIX]];
2. assert non-empty;
3. execute every displayed route;
4. record post-route Pareto/classification/severe flags;
5. run Diagnostic 4 pairwise.

Canonical invariants:

- pressure receding: Quiet; Hold only where source-for-politics trade meaningful; no Joint/Emergency;
- threshold: Quiet; no Emergency; Joint only for known coalition value over Quiet; Hold under evidence/partner predicate;
- seizure: no known-futile Quiet/Joint; late Quiet pruned when Joint strictly dominates; Emergency pruned whenever legal Quiet/Joint provides known denial and when Emergency cannot hold but Hold can; Emergency remains last-ditch when no better route exists;
- clean Quiet uses the **separate #100 tactical-warning product**, not broad preparation assessment.

Exhaustive expectation:

- zero empty route sets;
- zero displayed pairwise-dominated route pairs.

Also prove C5 attribution use removes Hold, source exposure severe, Emergency never clean Strategic Success, each final family useful somewhere viable and no final route universal.

## 17. Intent relevance

Vary one standing-intent answer with other inputs fixed. Every answer changes a reachable recommendation/reason or delegated consequence somewhere; require at least one real conflict between intent fields.

## 18. Adversary responsiveness / signal reachability / isolation

Using #99 + [[37A-COALITION-TO-RAVELLAN-SIGNAL-MATRIX]] prove authorised observation responsiveness, private-state isolation, normal reachability of CF-1/T-1, GP-1 and C6 strong-denial paths, deterministic coalescing, no contradictory signals, visible/quiet C5 divergence, C2–C4-only reserve exhaustion, and no dead C5 exhaustion observation.

## 19. Composed-command integrity

Prove complete package construction, C2 coordination, C5 channel/tempo, withdrawn+Honour, issue-order invariance, exceptional authority and source-sensitive Hold/Use trade.

## 20. Snowball / trivial late game

Flag early-success branches that remove later trade-offs: uniform advice, inelastic interventions, irrelevant recovery/information or collapsed route outcomes. Never add rubber-banding automatically.

## 21. Agenda collision

Report per cycle issue count, credible personal alternatives, intervention budget and elastic alternatives. C3/C5 must expose >2 credible personal-intervention candidates in promised histories; fake/dominated collision fails.

## 22. Dead state / observation

Every persisted field/evidence/observation needs downstream policy/state/legality/readout/debrief/replay-integrity purpose. C5 reserve-exhaustion remains absent as canonical regression.

## Rejection conditions

Reject #107 if it creates a scalar fun/balance score, ignores intervention opportunity or severe/source cost, collapses assessment/warning/public-case into one metric, judges information by evidence count, trains a gameplay agent, treats oracle truth as live recommendation truth, repairs invalid sampled packages, preserves dead state for symmetry, allows exceptional authority through Delegate, leaves package ties to array order, feeds diagnostics into gameplay, changes Ravellan dynamically to pass metrics or claims machine evidence passes the human gate.
