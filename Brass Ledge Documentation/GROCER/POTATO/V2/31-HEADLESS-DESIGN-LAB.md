# Headless Design Laboratory

Backlink: [[README]]

This is the implementation authority for #107. It falsifies broken structure before browser/human time is spent. It cannot certify fun.

[[27-KESTREL-TERMINAL-MATRIX]], [[37A-COALITION-TO-RAVELLAN-SIGNAL-MATRIX]] and [[39-KESTREL-CROSS-SYSTEM-COMPOSITION]] supply final composed invariants.

## Machine / human boundary

The lab may determine:

- seed fairness/viability;
- policy and local-package dominance;
- fake/inelastic choices;
- intervention/delegation value;
- strategy/intent separation;
- information/capability/target relevance;
- recovery reachability;
- adversary signal reachability/isolation;
- complete-package consistency;
- terminal-route completeness/non-dominance;
- dead state/observation;
- structural snowball/triviality.

It may not determine felt ownership, tension, regret, real-reading comprehension or voluntary replay desire.

## Deterministic policy cohort

- `delegate-all` — accept authoritative staff recommendation everywhere.
- `intervene-all-where-legal` — attempt maximum intervention use through a fixed authored preference order among non-recommended alternatives, always constructing a complete legal package.
- `recovery-first` — prefer authored recovery.
- `deterrence-first` — prefer visible/physical denial and forward readiness.
- `intelligence-first` — understanding-oriented intent, scheduled Lattice and legitimate information-producing courses.
- `coalition-first` — partner-oriented intent, consultation/authority/commitment preservation.
- `random-valid` — enumerate complete legal command packages through the authoritative validator and choose deterministically/uniformly; never sample issue choices then repair.

Generic policies exercise the 81 standing-intent combinations where specified/practical; fixed-strategy probes report fixed intent honestly.

## Oracle frontier

Test-only hidden-truth exploration may enumerate feasible legal continuations and non-dominated terminal frontier.

It never defines a canonical best move, staff advice or scalar optimum and never enters live gameplay.

## Outcome relation

Primary Pareto axes:

- Beacon security;
- partner consent/access;
- reserve readiness;
- commitment integrity.

Retain terminal classification + explicit severe-cost flags separately. No weighted score.

For within-cycle local comparisons also retain player-authority opportunity cost:

- normal interventions consumed;
- remaining normal intervention budget;
- other same-cycle legal packages/options foreclosed or preserved.

This is action-space opportunity cost, not another success score.

# Diagnostics

## 1. Seed viability / oracle fairness

Require:

- no forced hard loss before C6 without earlier meaningful counterplay;
- materially different non-oracle strategies reach C6;
- multiple non-dominated terminal profiles across viable seeds;
- promised costly recovery remains reachable through C5;
- oracle frontier does not prove unavailable hidden truth was necessary to avoid an otherwise unavoidable defeat.

## 2. Policy dominance

Across identical viable comparison sets, A dominates B only when A is no worse on all Pareto axes, no worse in terminal classification/severe-cost state where applicable and strictly better somewhere.

Flag universal dominance by Delegate-all, Intervene-all or one strategy family. Never “fix” by randomising/rubber-banding Ravellan.

## 3. Decision Elasticity

From identical verified pre-state, branch every authored consequential alternative.

Elastic differences may appear in:

- persistent state;
- HQ evidence/assessment;
- agenda/recommendation/dissent;
- legal action/capability/final-route space;
- authorised Ravellan observation/policy path;
- terminal Pareto/classification/severe state.

Prose-only divergence is fake/inelastic.

## 4. Player-safe local course/package dominance

A local decision cannot be judged as an isolated order effect when selecting it may consume one of only two personal interventions.

For one player-safe pre-state:

1. group hidden histories compatible with the same legitimate player information;
2. compare complete legal command packages that differ at the focal decision while holding other choices equivalent where possible;
3. include focal intervention cost and foreclosed/preserved same-cycle alternatives;
4. simulate every compatible hidden reality through normal authoritative transitions.

Compare:

- terminal Pareto vector;
- terminal classification;
- explicit severe-cost flags;
- intervention/action-space opportunity cost;
- later legal action/recovery/capability space;
- information value as a specific evidence/action-space effect.

Flag A dominated by B only when B is no worse across every compatible hidden reality **and** no worse in intervention/action-space cost, strictly better somewhere, and A has no compensating information/capability/recovery value.

Do not flag a legitimate risky course that wins in some compatible hidden realities and loses in others.

Explicitly test:

- C2 quiet / visible / reroute shipping;
- C2 silence / joint warning / commander-only accusation under partner-oriented and non-partner-oriented intent;
- C3 routine contact / reassurance under both recommendation baselines;
- C4 recover / quiet prepare / visible press;
- C5 quiet / visible / hold Beacon;
- C5 Honour / Act / Concession complete packages where comparable;
- C5 Hold / commander-only Use attribution;
- every displayed C6 route in the same safe terminal state.

## 5. Exceptional commander authority

Exactly these three courses are `requiresIntervention = true`:

1. C2 `public-accusation`;
2. C4 `request-partner-liaison`;
3. C5 `use-attribution`.

Across all relevant standing-intent combinations prove:

- none can ever become a staff recommendation or execute through Delegate;
- each consumes exactly one normal intervention when selected;
- visible-deterrence style cannot auto-accuse;
- understanding priority cannot auto-request liaison;
- partner priority/style cannot auto-use/burn the attribution source;
- no fourth Kestrel course accidentally acquires the flag.

## 6. Political command-by-exception baselines

Prove:

- C1 informal liaison is the final Political baseline unless standing partner direction selects formal consultation;
- C2 silence is the baseline unless standing partner direction selects joint warning;
- C3 routine contact is the baseline unless standing partner direction selects reassurance;
- C5 attribution staff baseline is always Hold.

A positive political action that requires intervention must not falsely dominate its baseline merely because its direct state effect is beneficial; intervention opportunity cost is part of Diagnostic 4.

## 7. C5 package recommendation / protected boundary

Using [[39-KESTREL-CROSS-SYSTEM-COMPOSITION]] prove:

- untouched all-Delegate package is legal in every reachable C5 state;
- package-level `partner-consultation` protected boundary is evaluated against complete viable packages, not only the authority sub-issue;
- rapid formal channel can make Honour compatible with immediate visible reinforcement;
- protected partner consultation prevents staff auto-Act when a non-sensitive or coordinated package can preserve the boundary;
- legal Concession may preserve consultation while buying immediate authority;
- when partner consultation is not protected, aggressive/Beacon-oriented standing direction may legitimately produce staff `act-then-inform`, with explicit authority/breach reasons;
- if no immediate-authority course is legal, visible reinforcement remains player-legal but staff recomputes among quiet/hold;
- no hidden/random compatibility repair exists.

### Final C5 Political authority tie

After package feasibility, protected-boundary, priority, style, tolerated-cost and commitment filtering, if more than one immediate-authority course remains:

- compatible `honour-consultation` → **Honour > Concession > Act**;
- Honour absent + partner `conditional|withdrawn` → **Concession > Act**;
- Honour absent + partner `cooperative|uneasy` → **Act > Concession**.

This is final professional tie-breaking only. Earlier command/commitment filters always take precedence.

No array order, lexical order, seed or randomness may resolve this tie.

## 8. Intervention / delegation value

Report opportunities, interventions used, downstream elasticity and real delegated cost.

Flag if always spending both or never intervening is weakly better in every comparable state, or if many overrides are mechanically empty.

## 9. Strategy separation

Intelligence-, coalition-, deterrence- and recovery-first must diverge on viable seeds across some of:

- Lattice/liaison;
- partner/promise/authority;
- Beacon preparation/exposure;
- reserve;
- evidence/attribution timing;
- Ravellan observation/policy path;
- final route/Pareto/classification.

## 10. Information value

Information matters when it changes assessment, advice, attribution/route availability, legal action-space or later rational choices.

It need not always improve outcome. Flag legitimate information that never matters while oracle truth is necessary to avoid defeat.

## 11. Capability / target relevance

Lattice must change legal information/action space and create at least one later elastic choice unavailable without it.

Require at least one viable non-Lattice non-defeat history.

At least two Lattice targets from the same reachable pre-task state must create different downstream mechanics. Reroute monitoring and liaison remain narrower/cost-different alternatives.

## 12. Recovery reachability

From every reachable C1–C5 non-terminal state test promised counterplay for reserve, partner, Beacon, missed Lattice and information weakness.

Flag recovery that cannot change the threatened state or is costless/dominant.

## 13. Terminal route completeness / dominance

For every abstract/reachable C6 player-known state:

1. derive route set from [[27-KESTREL-TERMINAL-MATRIX]];
2. assert route set is non-empty;
3. execute every displayed route;
4. record post-route Pareto/classification/severe flags;
5. run Diagnostic 4 pairwise among displayed routes.

Canonical invariants:

- pressure receding: Quiet; Hold only where source-for-politics trade is meaningful; no Joint/Emergency;
- threshold: Quiet; no Emergency; Joint only where it adds player-known coalition value over Quiet; Hold under its evidence/partner predicate;
- seizure: no known-futile Quiet/Joint; late Quiet pruned when Joint strictly dominates; Emergency pruned whenever legal Quiet/Joint already provides known denial and when Emergency cannot hold but Hold can; Emergency remains last-ditch/best-effort when no better route exists.

Canonical exhaustive abstract-state expectation:

- **zero empty route sets**;
- **zero displayed pairwise-dominated route pairs** under the player-safe Pareto + classification + severe-cost relation.

Also prove C5 attribution use removes Hold, source exposure is severe, Emergency is never clean Strategic Success, every final family is useful in at least one viable state, and no final route is universal.

## 14. Intent relevance

Vary one standing-intent answer at a time with other inputs fixed.

Every answer must alter at least one reachable recommendation/reason or delegated consequence somewhere. Require at least one real conflict between intent fields.

## 15. Adversary responsiveness / signal reachability / isolation

Using #99 + [[37A-COALITION-TO-RAVELLAN-SIGNAL-MATRIX]] prove:

- authorised observations change policy where the matrix permits;
- changing private HQ/coalition state with the observation set fixed leaves Ravellan unchanged;
- CF-1/T-1, GP-1 and C6 strong-denial paths are normally reachable;
- same-value signals coalesce deterministically;
- no legal package creates contradictory same-ID signals;
- visible/quiet C5 reinforcement diverge through discovery;
- reserve-exhaustion observation exists only from qualifying C2–C4 history with a later normal consumer;
- C5 reserve use emits no dead exhaustion observation.

## 16. Composed-command integrity

Prove complete legal package construction, C2 coordination, C5 channel/tempo, withdrawn+Honour, issue-order invariance, exact exceptional authority and source-sensitive Hold/Use trade.

## 17. Snowball / trivial late game

Flag early-success branches that remove later trade-offs: uniform advice, inelastic interventions, irrelevant recovery or collapsed route outcomes.

Never add rubber-banding automatically.

## 18. Agenda collision

Report per cycle:

- issue count;
- credible personal alternatives;
- intervention budget;
- mechanically elastic alternatives.

C3/C5 must expose >2 credible personal-intervention candidates in promised histories. Collision manufactured by fake/dominated choices fails.

## 19. Dead state / observation

Every persisted field/evidence/observation needs a downstream policy/state/legality/readout/debrief/replay-integrity purpose.

Canonical regression: C5 reserve-exhaustion observation remains absent because no later Kestrel Ravellan policy can read it.

# Counterfactual integrity

Counterfactual branches clone verified state/revision, use normal authoritative sim, never mutate the source run, remain deterministic and remain diagnostic-only.

Must deliberately detect:

- delegate-all / intervene-all dominance;
- inelastic choice;

- risky-but-non-dominated hidden-state choice (must not false-positive);

- irrelevant intent / strategy sameness;
- adversary-private / collection-posture leak;
- commander-only course accidentally delegated;

- doomed hidden seed;

## Rejection conditions

Reject #107 if it creates a scalar fun/balance score, ignores intervention opportunity cost or severe/source cost, trains a gameplay agent, treats oracle truth as live recommendation truth, repairs invalid sampled packages, preserves dead state for symmetry, allows exceptional authority through Delegate, leaves package ties to array order, feeds diagnostics into gameplay, changes Ravellan dynamically to pass metrics or claims machine evidence passes the human gate.
