# V2 Six-Cycle Vertical Slice

Backlink: [[README]]

This is the concise Kestrel summary. Exact implementation authority lives in the narrow numbered contracts.

Agents do not fill missing mechanics by inference.

# 1. Setting and player role

Kestrel Strait is a six-cycle laboratory about commanding a coalition HQ responsible for the Beacon Channel relay. Ravellan wants coalition control to become untenable through physical seizure, political access collapse or costly overreaction.

The commander sets four standing directions:

- what matters most;
- what staff must not spend without asking;
- which temporary cost is tolerable;
- how HQ normally behaves when several courses remain.

Staff runs the headquarters by default. The prototype allows two normal personal interventions per cycle. Ownership comes from direction + later consequence, not approving every staff action.

Exactly three courses are always commander-only:

- C2 public accusation;
- C4 partner liaison;
- C5 public attribution use.

# 2. Intelligence in one paragraph

HQ never receives a hidden-state answer or confidence meter.

From bounded evidence, Intelligence separately derives:

- **intent assessment** — what the pressure most likely means;
- **tactical warning** — whether there is current physical movement worth acting on;
- **public-case basis** — whether a directional claim is diagnostic and independently corroborated enough for source-sensitive public use.

These can disagree and use different currency windows. An old physical report may still inform assessment/public case after it is too stale to provide current warning.

#100 persists none of these. Exact semantics/state-space/catalog are `23/23A/23B/23C/23D`.

# 3. Cycle 1 — Ownership

Issues:

- Beacon watch;
- partner consultation;
- Lattice protection.

Ordinary watch preserves reserve but is detectably weak. Reinforcement improves coverage and strains reserve.

Formal consultation is not Political’s universal default. If selected, it creates exactly this promise:

> Consult the partner before a major visible military escalation or public attribution.

While active, it supplies the rapid channel used by C2/C5.

Lattice protection begins an exact three-window build; missing one scheduled advance makes maturity unreachable.

# 4. Cycle 2 — Consequence

Shipping:

- quiet escort — limited disruption, weaker visible denial;
- visible patrol surge — reserve-heavy denial;
- reroute and monitor — greater civilian/political disruption for a C3 auxiliary clue.

Political posture:

- silence;
- joint non-attributive warning;
- commander-only public accusation.

C2 partner effects derive atomically from the complete package. Active channel + joint warning coordinates visible surge. Without coordination, visible surge breaches an active consultation promise. Reroute and accusation have separate political contributions and may compound.

# 5. Cycle 3 — Doubt

Routine coverage creates two bounded signposts:

- logistics anomaly → preparation indicator;
- combat elements apparently dispersed **within routine coverage** → coercion indicator.

Together: `unclear / conflicted`.

Focused staging can divert Beacon coverage to obtain a C4 physical report. It never reads hidden posture and never consumes the stronger future Lattice landing question.

C3 also offers reserve posture, partner reassurance where relevant and final Lattice protection.

# 6. Cycle 4 — Payoff

Ravellan’s visible pressure pattern changes ambiguously. Situation prose may vary, but is never parsed into intelligence evidence.

Operational choice:

- recover reserve — improve endurance, worsen exposure;
- prepare Beacon quietly — improve/prepare Beacon at reserve cost;
- press visible advantage — overt denial at reserve cost, partner deterioration and active-promise breach.

A focused buildup result is:

- preparation indicator;
- warning current C4–C5;
- assessment/public evidence through C6.

Without a refreshed physical look, its warning is too stale for clean C6 reaction.

If Lattice matured, HQ automatically selects one unused target under standing priority; player may retarget at zero normal intervention. No no-task option.

Without Lattice, commander-only liaison is narrower, costs one intervention and creates an obligation.

# 7. Lattice information identity

Operational Lattice asks exactly one target in C4 and a **different unused** target in C5:

- `landing-force-staging` — diagnostic preparation + physical warning when concentration exists;
- `auxiliary-tasking` — strongest diagnostic coercion path;
- `operational-sequence` — sustained preparation/public-case evidence without direct warning.

Same-target C5 retask is illegal because C6 adds no normal Ravellan action/preparation transition; it would return the same source fact in every architecture history.

A focused-positive C4 report never removes landing eligibility. Lattice landing is diagnostically stronger and refreshes warning through C6.

# 8. Cycle 5 — Pain

Collision:

- wider intent assessment;
- separate current warning;
- Beacon posture;
- reserve condition;
- partner authority/tempo;
- optional current corroborated public claim + unspent source;
- second Lattice target if operational.

Complete package is atomic.

Partner authority resolves `none|joint|unilateral|concession` from `pending`.

- Honour preserves commitments and can create C6 joint authority; same-cycle sensitive action needs the pre-existing rapid channel.
- Act Then Inform buys unilateral tempo at political/breach cost.
- Concession buys immediate coordinated authority at severe cost.
- Withdrawn partner may Honour without recovering access.

Operations’ final professional tie, after command filters:

1. current warning usable → quiet reinforcement > visible > hold;
2. else preparation assessment → quiet > visible > hold;
3. else → hold > quiet > visible.

Protected reserve may still override that tie and create visible staff tension.

Current public attribution issue appears only when #100 has a diagnostic + independently corroborated directional case and source is unspent. Staff Holds. Commander-only Use freezes the exact claim/two-source basis and exposes the source. Holding persists nothing and does not guarantee C6 availability.

# 9. Cycle 6 — Reckoning

Exact order:

```text
hidden R6 decision
→ C5 Lattice result from latest-normal facts
→ final pre-manifestation #100 snapshot/current public case
→ safe overt crisis
→ legal routes
```

Player sees only:

- seizure underway;
- threshold confrontation;
- pressure receding.

Intelligence is explicitly labelled as what HQ knew immediately before the confrontation became overt.

Final routes:

- Quiet Denial;
- Joint Visible Denial where coalition/physical state adds real value;
- Emergency Mobilisation as fallback only;
- Hold And Expose where current corroborated case, unspent source, partner access and physical predicates make it meaningful.

Clean Quiet against seizure requires prepared denial + controlled exposure + **current** warning. A stale focused report or broad preparation assessment is insufficient.

Final effects mutate state before classification.

Outcomes:

- Strategic Success;
- Costly Success;
- Political Defeat;
- Operational Defeat.

# 11. Fairness / recovery

Before C6, serious deterioration retains costly counterplay for reserve, partner, Beacon, missed Lattice and information weakness where temporally useful.

No seed may force hard loss before terminal without earlier meaningful legal countermeasure. Information helps but is not the only hidden-truth key to survival.

# 12. Emotional arc

1. Ownership — “I chose how this HQ should behave.”
2. Consequence — “It followed that direction and spent something.”
3. Doubt — “The evidence points in different directions.”
4. Payoff — “My institutional/collection choice changed what I can know/do.”
5. Pain — “I cannot keep warning, certainty, authority, readiness and protection simultaneously.”
6. Reckoning — “This crisis is testing the campaign I built.”
