# Setting

Kestrel Strait is a six-cycle laboratory about commanding a coalition HQ responsible for the Beacon Channel relay. Ravellan wants coalition control to become untenable before reinforcement is politically feasible, through physical seizure or political access collapse.

The commander sets four standing directions: what matters most, what staff must not spend without asking, which temporary cost is tolerable, and how HQ normally behaves when several reasonable options remain.

Staff runs the headquarters by default. The prototype allows two personal interventions per cycle. Standing direction can therefore cause consequential staff action without a click; ownership comes from direction + later consequence, not mandatory approvals.

# Intelligence model in one paragraph

HQ does not receive a hidden-state estimate or confidence meter.

From bounded evidence, Intelligence separately derives:

- **intent assessment** — what HQ thinks the pressure means;
- **tactical warning** — whether HQ has a direct actionable physical preparation signpost;
- **public-case basis** — whether evidence can support a source-sensitive public case.

Those can disagree. HQ may think preparation without usable warning, or have usable physical warning while wider intent remains conflicted.

All three are **pure derived readouts** under #100, reconstructed from trusted history; none is persisted campaign state. Later #101 alone persists the one-shot attribution opportunity.

# Six-cycle pressure arc

## Cycle 1 — Ownership

Issues:

- Beacon watch;
- partner consultation;
- Lattice protection.

Ordinary watch is detectably weak; reinforcement improves coverage but strains reserve. Formal consultation is not Political's universal default: partner-oriented standing direction can select it. It creates a real promise and a rapid consultation channel. Lattice protection begins the three-window institutional build.

The player is not forced to “fail one of three”; direction/delegation/intervention determine which real costs/commitments are accepted.

## Cycle 2 — Consequence

Ravellan's opening shipping pressure persists while the new hidden action adds bounded world effects.

Shipping:

- quiet escort — less disruption, weaker visible denial;
- visible patrol surge — reserve-heavy denial;
- reroute and monitor — greater civilian/political disruption for a persistent indicator-only auxiliary clue usable C3–C5.

Political posture:

- silence;
- joint non-attributive warning;
- commander-only unilateral public accusation.

Active C1 formal channel + joint warning can coordinate visible surge and avoid its partner penalty. Public accusation always damages partner; active promise additionally breaches.

## Cycle 3 — Doubt

Routine HQ coverage produces the same bounded competing signposts across hidden openings:

- logistics anomaly → preparation indicator;
- observed combat elements appear dispersed → coercion indicator.

These are **not omniscient global truth**. Together they produce `unclear + conflicted`.

Issues can include forward reserve prep vs hold, focused staging collection vs maintain coverage, partner reassurance where relevant, and final Lattice protection.

Focused collection reads only the authorised physical preparation fact at result time, never hidden posture.

## Cycle 4 — Payoff

Ravellan's visible pressure pattern changes ambiguously according to actual action. Situation prose can vary, but action-specific prose never becomes extra analytic evidence.

Operational Lattice unlocks one named Task Collection outside the normal intervention budget. Without it, partner liaison is narrower, commander-only, costs one intervention and creates an obligation.

Operational choice:

- recover reserve — endurance at Beacon-exposure cost;
- prepare Beacon quietly — prepare/improve Beacon at readiness cost and become detectably targeted;
- press visible advantage — overt denial at reserve/political risk.

A C3 focused result can create **usable physical warning** without magically resolving the entire intent estimate.

## Cycle 5 — Pain

The collision is now explicitly multi-dimensional:

- what HQ broadly assesses Ravellan is doing;
- whether HQ has separate usable tactical warning;
- partner authority/tempo;
- reserve condition;
- optional one-shot public attribution opportunity derived/persisted through #101 from a legitimate public-case basis.

The complete final-order package is atomic; issue order cannot decide state.

Partner authority resolves `none|joint|unilateral|concession` from opening `pending`.

- Honour preserves commitments and can create C6 joint authority; same-cycle sensitive action through Honour requires active rapid C1 channel.
- Act Then Inform buys unilateral tempo at political/breach cost.
- Political Concession buys immediate coordinated authority at severe cost.
- Withdrawn partner can still Honour without forced concession, preserving integrity but not access.

Visible vs quiet Beacon reinforcement are distinct. Visible emits targeted discovery/deterrence signal; quiet avoids that public exposure.

### Warning-sensitive Operations

After the commander's higher standing-direction/commitment filters:

- usable tactical warning makes Operations professionally prefer reinforcement even if wider intent remains conflicted;
- preparation assessment without warning also biases preparation, but does **not** gain the clean-warning terminal benefit;
- a protected reserve red line can still override the professional tie and should create visible staff tension/reasoning.

### Attribution

Public attribution is a one-shot source-sensitive opportunity. #100's internal public-case basis does not directly create the C5 course; #101 persists the legitimate unspent opportunity. Use is commander-only, consumes it and exposes the protected source; Hold preserves it for possible C6 Hold And Expose.

## Cycle 6 — Reckoning

The player sees only overt crisis family:

- `seizure-underway`;
- `threshold-confrontation`;
- `pressure-receding`.

Prior hidden posture/preparation history stays hidden until debrief.

Final route set is pruned using overt crisis + player-known state:

- Quiet Denial;
- Joint Visible Denial where joint/physical state gives real value;
- Emergency Mobilisation as fallback only;
- Hold And Expose where unspent attribution/access/physical predicates make source expenditure meaningful.

Against seizure, a **clean** Quiet response requires prepared denial + controlled exposure + the separate #100 usable-warning product. Broad preparation assessment alone is insufficient.

Final route effects mutate authoritative state before Pareto/classification.

Outcomes: Strategic Success, Costly Success, Political Defeat, Operational Defeat.

# Persistent versus derived state

## Persistent Kestrel campaign/world state

- Beacon exposure/preparation;
- reserve condition;
- partner consent;
- consultation promise/channel;
- partner authority;
- political concession;
- liaison obligation;
- Lattice investment/task truth once #102 implements it;
- attribution opportunity/source-consumption state once #101 implements it;
- hidden Ravellan posture/preparation/observations under #99.

## Derived readout, not persisted #100 state

- HQ evidence history;
- intent assessment;
- tactical warning;
- public-case basis;
- Intelligence-Chief brief/change/gap/watch-for refs.

This distinction is replay architecture, not merely presentation.

# Fairness / recovery

Before C6, serious deterioration retains costly counterplay where promised:

- reserve → consolidation;
- partner → consultation/concession depending objective/history;
- Beacon → preparation/reinforcement;
- missed Lattice → narrower liaison;
- poor information → focused/Lattice/liaison routes where temporally useful.

No seed may create unavoidable pre-terminal hard loss without earlier meaningful legal countermeasure. Information must help but cannot be the only hidden-truth key to survival.

# Emotional arc

1. Ownership — “I chose how this HQ should behave.”
2. Consequence — “It followed that direction and spent something.”
3. Doubt — “The evidence points in different directions.”
4. Payoff — “The institution/collection choice changed what kind of information/action I have.”
5. Pain — “I cannot have warning, certainty, political authority, readiness and protection all at once.”
6. Reckoning — “This crisis is testing the campaign I built.”
