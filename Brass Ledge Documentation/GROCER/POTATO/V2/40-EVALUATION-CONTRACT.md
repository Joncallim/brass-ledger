# V2 Evaluation Contract

Backlink: [[README]]

## Machine-provable contract

The implementation must prove deterministic replay; V1 compatibility; save and
import rejection for mismatched rulesets; server stale-write protection; world
truth isolation; reasoned recommendation structure; strategy/action reachability;
no legal always-intervene or always-delegate dominance across authored seeds;
Lattice changing information/action space; adversary response to observable
signals rather than score; multiple final-crisis solutions across viable history;
and a costly recovery path before terminal crisis.

The future headless laboratory runs stable deterministic policies: delegate-all,
intervene-all-where-legal, recovery-first, deterrence-first, intelligence-first,
**coalition-first**, random-valid, and test-only oracle. Coalition-first protects
consultation/partner authority and prefers joint/coherent action when legally
available; it is a diagnostic strategy probe, not staff/player AI. Oracle is a
diagnostic upper bound only and must never be used in staff/player paths. It
reports seed viability, dominance, Decision Elasticity/fake-choice findings,
early collapse/snowball, strategy separation, capability relevance, final-choice
sameness, adversary responsiveness, and whether information changes action
quality. Comparison is laboratory-only Pareto comparison across Beacon security,
partner consent, reserve readiness, and commitment integrity. Earned intelligence
is reported as a diagnostic rather than a success axis; capability relevance
requires a changed legal decision or a non-intelligence terminal effect. This is
not a player score, staff utility, or adversary input. A policy is dominated only
when another is at least as good on every axis and strictly better on one over
the same viable seed set. Prototype viability requires no forced hard loss before
the final crisis, materially different non-oracle strategies reaching cycle six,
at least two non-dominated terminal profiles across authored seeds, and a costly
recovery path through cycle five. Oracle uses hidden truth only to diagnose seed
or information unfairness and never enters staff/player logic.

## Human-only contract

Fresh players must describe what they caused, name a next-turn plan, understand
why a consequence followed, report a meaningful regret/vindication or surprise,
and voluntarily want another turn or a different strategy. Facilitators record
verbatim evidence; an AI cannot infer it from telemetry.

The formative smoke uses three fresh players. If at least two cannot explain
major consequences, cannot form a next-cycle plan, or show no voluntary desire
to continue, stop and redesign before the formal gate. The formal gate uses
eight fresh players: at least 6/8 want another turn/run, identify a significant
personal cause, and state an unprompted forward strategy; at least 5/8 report
meaningful regret, vindication, tension, surprise, or payoff; and at least 6/8
use ownership language. A repeated severe qualitative failure overrides counts.

**No AI agent may self-certify a human fun gate.** A green build, an oracle win,
or synthetic playtest is never a human-fun pass. Failed human hypotheses route
to a focused issue, not polish or retrospective reinterpretation.
