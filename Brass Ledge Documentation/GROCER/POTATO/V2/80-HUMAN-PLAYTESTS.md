# V2 Human Playtests

Backlink: [[README]]

Use fresh players who have not been coached on V2. Run the complete six-cycle
plain-text slice without interruption; do not facilitate a preferred strategy or
reveal Ravellan’s hidden posture. Record spontaneous explanations, planning,
confusion, disengagement, and desire to stop without prompting during the run.

At the end ask: what did you personally cause; what would you change on replay;
which uncertainty mattered; whether the final crisis reflected the run; and
whether you wanted another turn/replay. Offer an actual optional replay without
coaching and record uptake separately from prompted interest. Record strategy,
cycle reached, major orders, outcome, comprehension, ownership, causal
understanding, emotional response, spontaneous continuation/replay, prompted
interest, and actual replay uptake. Anonymise evidence below.

| Tester | Prior experience | Spontaneous causal/plan evidence | Regret/vindication | Prompted interest | Actual replay uptake | Finding / follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | | | |

This protocol is a human gate only. No synthetic run, agent report, or automated
test may populate a passing conclusion.
