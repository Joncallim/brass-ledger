### Prompted post-run responses

Keep separate from spontaneous evidence. Follow [[35-HUMAN-PLAYTEST-HARNESS]] order.

Open questions:

- What happened in your campaign?
- What did you personally cause or set in motion?
- What were you trying to do by the end?
- Which uncertainty mattered most?
- What did you regret / feel vindicated about?
- Did final confrontation connect to earlier play?
- What would you change on replay?
- What felt like paperwork?
- Where was game confusing/under-informative?
- Did any option feel fake/trap?

Only after open questions, if needed, record neutral intelligence follow-ups from 35:

- **Did “we think this” versus “we have directly seen something worth acting on” feel distinct?**
- **Did collection questions feel strategically different or basically equivalent?**
- **Did Intelligence feel too certain, too vague, or like it already knew the answer? Why?**
