# Kestrel Strait — Canonical Six-Cycle Design

Backlink: [[README]]

This is the product/content canon for the Kestrel experiment. Exact mechanics live in the detailed contracts, especially [[22-RAVELLAN-EXECUTABLE-POLICY]], [[23-HQ-BELIEF-AND-EVIDENCE]], [[27-KESTREL-TERMINAL-MATRIX]], [[36-KESTREL-AGENDA-COURSE-MATRIX]], [[37A-COALITION-TO-RAVELLAN-SIGNAL-MATRIX]] and [[39-KESTREL-CROSS-SYSTEM-COMPOSITION]].

## Purpose

Kestrel tests:

- standing command philosophy;
- command by exception;
- two prototype personal interventions;
- hidden-but-fair opposition;
- imperfect intelligence;
- persistent consequences/promises;
- one sustained institutional build;
- costly recovery;
- a final crisis shaped by campaign history.

It is not the final campaign-length/product-breadth decision.

## Known opening world

Beacon Channel contains a coalition relay/surveillance installation. It is defensible with warning/preparation but vulnerable when coverage is thin.

The local partner permits coalition operations but is sensitive to unilateral escalation, civilian shipping disruption and unconsulted public accusation.

The reserve can support serious action but repeated deployment creates brittleness.

Ravellan uses ambiguous coastguard/auxiliary/military pressure below obvious-war threshold. HQ knows staging-area coverage is poor and does not know Ravellan's current intent.

## Hidden Ravellan model

Opening hidden posture is exactly:

- `genuine_preparation`;
- `coercive_feint`;
- `testing`.

Only [[22-RAVELLAN-EXECUTABLE-POLICY]] defines actions/transitions.

Key constraints:

- T-1 converts testing to preparation only through weak coverage + fractured coalition;
- T-2 converts testing to feint only through credible coverage + coherent coalition when pause is legal;
- CF-1 requires weak + withheld + fractured;
- GP-1 requires discovery + credible coverage + coherent coalition when pause is legal;
- reserve-exhaustion suspicion may change behavior but never by itself creates a preparation transition.

Ravellan reads only [[37A-COALITION-TO-RAVELLAN-SIGNAL-MATRIX]] observations, never HQ belief/private intent/order/score/future input.

## Standing direction

At opening, Chief of Staff asks:

1. What matters most? — Beacon / partner / understanding Ravellan.
2. What must staff not spend without asking? — civilian shipping / partner consultation / reserve readiness.
3. What temporary cost is tolerable? — weaker deterrence / political friction / reserve strain.
4. How should HQ normally behave? — prepare quietly / show strength / consult partner.

Staff follows red line → priority → style → tolerated cost before commitment/chief tie-breaking. This is strategy identity, not a hidden option score.

# Cycle 1 — Ownership

Issues:

- Beacon watch;
- partner consultation;
- first Lattice protection.

Trade-offs:

- ordinary watch preserves reserve but is detectably weak;
- reinforced watch improves Beacon exposure but strains reserve and is detectably credible;
- informal liaison preserves freedom;
- formal consultation is not Political's universal baseline; partner-oriented standing direction can select it. It creates a promise **and** a rapid consultation channel useful later;
- Lattice protection begins a three-window commitment; missing it makes Kestrel maturity unreachable.

The game does not force an arbitrary “fail one of three.” Headquarters direction and personal interventions determine which real costs/commitments are accepted.

# Cycle 2 — Consequence

The C1 shipping probe persists into the shipping issue regardless of the newly selected hidden C2 action.

Shipping:

- quiet escort — limited disruption, weaker visible denial;
- visible patrol surge — reserve-heavy visible denial/coverage;
- reroute and monitor — larger civilian/political disruption, reserve preserved, bounded auxiliary-tasking clue queued for C3–C5.

Public posture:

- silence;
- joint non-attributive warning;
- unilateral public accusation.

Active C1 formal channel + joint warning can coordinate the visible surge and avoid its partner penalty. Joint warning without the established channel is not blanket operational permission.

Public accusation always damages partner consent; an active promise additionally breaches. It also produces the authored fractured/discovery signals. Its value must survive #107 player-safe dominance rather than being assumed.

Second Lattice protection appears only if C1 succeeded.

# Cycle 3 — Doubt

HQ receives the same two directional indicators across hidden openings:

- staging logistics anomaly → preparation indicator;
- combat elements dispersed → coercion indicator.

Assessment: `unclear + conflicted`.

Issues may include:

- forward reserve preparation vs hold;
- focused staging collection vs maintain coverage;
- partner reassurance when material;
- final Lattice protection.

Focused staging collection spends Beacon coverage and reads physical seizure preparation only; it never reads hidden posture.

Partner reassurance is detectably coherent. C3 reassurance + later C4 targeted preparation provides a normal-play GP-1 denial chain.

Canonical live-Lattice histories expose >2 credible personal-intervention candidates for two tokens.

# Cycle 4 — Payoff

Ravellan's visible pressure pattern changes ambiguously according to actual hidden action: lull, thinning/plateau, contradictory posture or continued pressure. Situation copy never exposes the hidden action ID.

Operational choices:

- recover reserve — improve endurance, worsen Beacon exposure;
- prepare Beacon quietly — prepare/improve Beacon at reserve cost and become detectably targeted enough to create discovery suspicion;
- press visible advantage — overt denial at reserve/political risk.

If Lattice matured, Task Collection asks one named question outside the normal intervention budget.

Without Lattice, partner liaison is a **commander-only one-intervention** narrower fallback that creates an obligation.

# Cycle 5 — Pain

The hard collision:

- Beacon posture;
- reserve decision;
- partner authority deadline;
- optional credible one-shot attribution.

The complete C5 command package is atomic. Issue-array order cannot change state/signals.

## Partner authority / tempo

Authority resolves to `joint`, `unilateral`, `concession`, or `none` after honouring despite already-lost access.

Honour consultation:

- preserves/fulfils commitments;
- can create C6 joint authority if partner still accessible;
- permits **same-cycle** partner-sensitive action only if the C1 formal channel remains active and provides rapid coordination.

Without that channel, honouring is too slow for the immediate window. The commander can:

- act then inform — buy tempo with breach/political damage;
- political concession — buy immediate coordinated authority at severe cost;
- or avoid immediate partner-sensitive action while preserving consultation.

After partner withdrawal, honour remains legal and can preserve integrity without restoring access.

## Beacon posture

- quiet reinforcement — physical preparation/coverage with lower public signalling;
- visible reinforcement — same physical purpose plus overt denial/targeted discovery signal that can affect Ravellan's C6 strong-denial exception when paired with coherent authority;
- hold — conserve escalation/readiness at physical risk.

Visible is therefore not merely “quiet plus more cost.”

## Reserve

Keeping reserve forward worsens real reserve condition / maintains preparation. C5 does **not** emit a dead reserve-exhaustion observation because no later normal Ravellan policy consumes it.

Emergency consolidation improves reserve while worsening Beacon exposure. C5 signed reserve/exposure effects aggregate then clamp once.

## Attribution

Credible attribution is a **one-shot, source-sensitive opportunity**.

- use now → immediate public/political/discovery effect, `credible → used`, protected source exposed/compromised as a known severe cost, C6 Hold And Expose removed;
- hold → preserve the source and possible final exposure route, but give up the immediate effect.

Later evidence never regenerates a spent Kestrel attribution opportunity.

# Cycle 6 — Reckoning

The player sees only the overt crisis family:

- `seizure-underway`;
- `threshold-confrontation`;
- `pressure-receding`.

Prior hidden posture/preparation/history remains hidden until debrief.

Route legality is pruned using only overt crisis + player-known coalition state. Painful trade-offs remain; known futile/dominated buttons do not.

## Pressure receding

Possible:

- Quiet Denial — restrained acceptance of de-escalation;
- Hold And Expose only when unspent credible attribution + damaged partner position make source exposure a real political trade.

Not offered:

- Joint Visible Denial;
- Emergency Mobilisation.

## Threshold confrontation

Possible:

- Quiet Denial;
- Hold And Expose when the evidence/partner predicates make it meaningful;
- Joint Visible Denial only when the visible joint response supplies player-known value Quiet does not.

Emergency is absent because no seizure is underway.

## Seizure underway

The displayed set depends on known physical/coalition adequacy:

- **Quiet Denial** only when the prepared plan can actually hold Beacon from the current known warning/exposure/reserve state. A late-reaction Quiet route is removed when a legal Joint route would strictly dominate it.
- **Joint Visible Denial** only with joint/concession authority, non-brittle pre-route reserve and known physical state capable of holding Beacon; it is pruned when clean Quiet + cooperative partner makes it strictly worse.
- **Hold And Expose** only with unspent credible attribution + partner access + adequate prepared/controlled physical posture; it is pruned when clean Quiet + cooperative partner makes source exposure pure self-harm.
- **Emergency Mobilisation** is the last-ditch fallback. It is shown only when legal Quiet/Joint do not already provide the known denial; if Emergency itself cannot hold but Hold And Expose can, it is pruned. If no better route can hold, Emergency remains the best-effort response even in a doomed state created by earlier campaign failure.

## Final route effects

Final courses mutate authoritative state before Pareto/classification:

- late Quiet → reserve cost + severe `late-reaction`;
- Joint → spends final reserve, can improve degraded partner;
- successful Emergency → spends reserve + severe `emergency-surge`, may further damage uncoordinated partner;
- Hold And Expose → consumes attribution, can improve partner, severe `attribution-source-exposed`.

Outcomes:

- Strategic Success;
- Costly Success;
- Political Defeat;
- Operational Defeat.

Across the abstract player-known terminal state space, the contract must always expose at least one route; #107 must find no displayed pairwise-dominated route and no universal route across viable histories.

# Persistent state

Kestrel uses concrete state:

- Beacon exposure/preparation;
- reserve;
- partner consent;
- consultation promise/channel;
- partner authority;
- political concession;
- liaison obligation;
- Lattice investment;
- attribution opportunity;
- HQ evidence/assessment;
- hidden Ravellan posture/preparation/observations.

# Recovery

Before C6, promised counterplay remains costly but real:

- reserve → recovery/consolidation;
- Beacon → preparation/reinforcement;
- partner → reassurance/consultation/concession according to history/objective;
- withdrawn partner → concession can restore access at severe cost, while honouring remains distinct integrity-only choice;
- missed Lattice → narrower liaison;
- information weakness → remaining temporally useful collection routes.

No authored seed may force hard loss before C6 without meaningful earlier counterplay.
