# Helena Ortiz — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We find the weak link after the plan is already signed off.

### beat: ortiz.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Someone says the partner will be there, or the lift will arrive, or the reserve is free. Fine. What do we do if one of those things isn't true?

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: ortiz.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I wanted partner confirmation before we moved on one operation.

### beat: ortiz.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The commander didn't give me the time. We built a thinner fallback and went. It worked. I learned the fallback doesn't need to be comfortable. It just needs to exist.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If the whole plan rests on one thing going right and we've got no reserve and no useful fallback, I'm not ready to commit.

### beat: ortiz.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  That's not caution. That's just letting one assumption own the whole plan.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I'm bad with late changes once I think the plan is settled.

### beat: ortiz.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Sometimes the late change is exactly what we need. My first reaction doesn't always make it easy for people to bring me that news.

prompt_id: interview.crisis-peer

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  In Plans, I'd have to watch the urge to turn everything into a sequence.

### beat: ortiz.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  I'm very comfortable with who moves when and what happens if they don't. Political purpose doesn't always line up that neatly.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Build around the bit we don't know.

### beat: ortiz.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If the plan still works when that part goes wrong, we don't need to wait for certainty.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I'd want a real recovery plan for next month before I spend the capacity now.

### beat: ortiz.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If the answer is just 'we'll deal with it later,' that's not much of a trade.
