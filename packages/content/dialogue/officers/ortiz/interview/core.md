# Helena Ortiz — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We discover dependencies after the plan is approved.

### beat: ortiz.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Someone says the partner will be there, the lift will arrive, or the reserve is available, but nobody owns the branch if one of those things fails.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: ortiz.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once wanted a partner confirmation before we moved.

### beat: ortiz.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The commander did not give me the time. We built a thinner branch and went. It worked. I learned that a branch does not need to be comfortable. It needs to be real.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If a plan has one point of failure, no reserve and no useful branch, I will tell you we are not ready to commit.

### beat: ortiz.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  That is not caution. That is handing the whole plan to one assumption.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I have very little patience for late changes after I think the plan is settled.

### beat: ortiz.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Sometimes the late change is exactly what reality requires. My first reaction can make it harder for people to bring me that news.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  In Plans I would worry about turning strategy into sequence.

### beat: ortiz.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  I am very comfortable with who moves when and what happens if they do not. Policy and political purpose do not always fit into that kind of order.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Build the branch around the uncertainty.

### beat: ortiz.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we can protect the failure case, we do not need to wait for certainty before moving.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: ortiz.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If we can name the recovery branch for next month, spending capacity now may be worth it.

### beat: ortiz.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If there is no branch and we simply hope the cost disappears, that is not a trade. It is a cliff.
