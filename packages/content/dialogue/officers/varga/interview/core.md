# Tomas Varga — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: varga.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Reports grow as they move up the chain.

### beat: varga.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  One person sees one convoy at one junction. By the third briefing it sounds like we watched the whole move.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: varga.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  Two reports. Same source underneath both. I was asked to call the location confirmed.

### beat: varga.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  I wouldn't use the word. The commander still planned against the location. Fine. At least he knew what we actually had.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: varga.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If the plan depends on a source seeing more than it could actually see, I'll say it.

### beat: varga.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  You can still act. Just don't make the source carry weight it never had.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: varga.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I sit on reporting too long.

### beat: varga.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  I want to know where it came from and what happened to it on the way up. Sometimes a useful warning gets here later because of that.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: varga.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Operations? That's a stretch for me.

### beat: varga.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  I know what field reporting can give an operator. I haven't spent a career running the whole operation. I'd probably give collection too much weight.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: varga.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Pick one question we can answer tonight.

### beat: varga.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Stage what we can pull back while we collect. We may not need a big answer.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: varga.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Known cost, uncertain gain? I'd be careful.

### beat: varga.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Take the cost when the advantage is real, not because the reporting might turn into something.
