# Claire Dubois — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: dubois.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We decide first, tell people later, then act surprised when they push back.

### beat: dubois.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we need someone else's help to make the plan work, I'd rather they hear it from us before the order lands.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: dubois.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once argued against cutting a partner out just to save a day.

### beat: dubois.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The commander did it anyway. We saved the day and lost their access for three weeks. I still think it was the wrong call, but I also learned there are days when you knowingly spend the relationship.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: dubois.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If the plan only works because a partner, employer, or outside agency does something nobody has actually asked them to do, I'll stop it.

### beat: dubois.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  That's not coordination. That's hope.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: dubois.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I leave hard conversations too late.

### beat: dubois.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  I keep thinking I can preserve the relationship and fix the problem quietly. By the time I finally say, 'this isn't working,' everyone else already knows.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: dubois.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  In Plans, I'd have to watch the instinct to keep everyone together.

### beat: dubois.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Sometimes the campaign needs a clean choice, even if somebody won't like it.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: dubois.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Move on the parts we own.

### beat: dubois.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Call the partner now about the rest. Waiting quietly doesn't preserve anything if they can already see us moving.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: dubois.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If next month's cost is trust, I'd take it seriously. That can last a lot longer than a month.

### beat: dubois.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If it's our own buffer, maybe we spend it. I care what kind of weakness we're creating.
