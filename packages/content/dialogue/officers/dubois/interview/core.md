# Claire Dubois — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: dubois.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We tell people after the decision and then call their reaction friction.

### beat: dubois.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we need somebody's help to carry the plan, they should normally hear about it before the order arrives.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: dubois.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once argued against bypassing a partner to save a day.

### beat: dubois.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The commander bypassed them. We saved the day and lost access for the next three weeks. I still think the call was wrong, but it taught me that sometimes the immediate day really is worth the relationship cost. You just need to know that is the trade.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: dubois.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If success depends on a partner, reserve employer or outside organisation that has not been asked, I will tell you the plan is not ready.

### beat: dubois.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Hope is not coordination.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: dubois.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I can wait too long to confront someone because I want to preserve the working relationship.

### beat: dubois.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  By the time I say 'this is no longer working,' everyone else may have known for a week.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: dubois.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  In Plans I would worry that I keep trying to preserve cooperation after the campaign needs a clean choice.

### beat: dubois.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  A relationship can be valuable and still not be worth protecting in every decision.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: dubois.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Act on the parts we own.

### beat: dubois.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Call the partner now on the parts we do not. Silence does not preserve the option if the other side sees us moving anyway.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: dubois.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If next month's weakness is trust with a partner, the cost can last much longer than a month.

### beat: dubois.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If it is our own internal buffer, maybe we spend it. The type of cost matters.
