# Miriam Chen — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: chen.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We split a problem into boxes and then forget the boxes still touch.

### beat: chen.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Shipping affects spares. Spares affect repairs. Repairs affect what we can put in the field. None of that is difficult by itself. The trouble is when we look at only one piece.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: chen.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  There was a plan to cut one supply route. I thought the first real effect would be on repair parts, not fuel.

### beat: chen.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The commander was watching fuel because that was the obvious number. I was looking further down the chain. The repair delay showed up first.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: chen.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If an order assumes one part changes and everything around it stays put, I'll want to check that.

### beat: chen.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Sometimes that assumption is fine. Sometimes it's the whole problem.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: chen.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I add too many caveats.

### beat: chen.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Usually because I've spotted another link and I'm worried I've missed one more. You can end up with a very accurate answer that nobody knows what to do with.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: chen.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I've done campaign planning, so Plans is a real option for me.

### beat: chen.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The risk is that I keep widening the problem. A planner eventually has to say, 'that's enough, this is the part we're acting on.'

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: chen.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Find the first thing that can actually break this week.

### beat: chen.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Protect that. We can keep working on the bigger picture without waiting to understand every part of it.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: chen.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I'd want to know whether the cost stops next month or starts feeding itself.

### beat: chen.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  One bad month may be worth it. A choice that makes every month after that harder is a different problem.
