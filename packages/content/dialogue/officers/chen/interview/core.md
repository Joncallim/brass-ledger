# Miriam Chen — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: chen.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We break connected problems into neat boxes, then forget the boxes still affect one another.

### beat: chen.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  A shipping decision changes repair parts. Repair delays change readiness. Readiness changes what we can promise a partner. None of those links is complicated on its own. Together they are the problem.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: chen.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once argued that cutting one supply route would hurt repair parts before it hurt fuel.

### beat: chen.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The commander focused on fuel because it was the obvious measure. We were both looking at the same system from different distances. The repair delay was the thing that mattered first.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: chen.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If an order assumes one part of the system changes while everything around it stays still, I will ask you to test that assumption.

### beat: chen.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Sometimes it is fine. Sometimes that is where the real risk sits.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: chen.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  When I am worried I have missed a link, I start adding caveats. Then I think of another link and add one more.

### beat: chen.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  The result can be perfectly accurate and almost useless. If you have to dig for my recommendation, I have not finished the job.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: chen.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I can lead Plans because I have actually done campaign planning.

### beat: chen.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  My worry is the same one I have in Intelligence: I can widen the problem faster than I narrow it. A planner has to choose where to stop looking.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: chen.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I would start with the first link that can actually break this week.

### beat: chen.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  The wider picture still matters, but we do not need to solve all of it before protecting that link.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: chen.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  A short-term gain is fine if the feedback stops there.

### beat: chen.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If it starts a cycle that keeps making next month worse, the cost is larger than the first number on the page.
