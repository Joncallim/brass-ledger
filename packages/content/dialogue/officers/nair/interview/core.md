# Priya Nair — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: nair.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We settle on one story too quickly.

### beat: nair.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Three strange facts might point to the same thing. Or they might just be three strange facts. I want at least one other explanation on the table before we fall in love with the first one.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: nair.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once pushed a pattern that looked like preparation for a major move.

### beat: nair.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The activity was real. My explanation wasn't. I'd spotted something useful and then got too attached to the story I'd built around it.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: nair.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If we're about to do something irreversible because of one explanation we still can't separate from another plausible one, I'll ask you to slow down.

### beat: nair.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  If the action makes sense under both, then I'm much less worried.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: nair.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I get defensive about a hypothesis I've spent too long on.

### beat: nair.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Especially when the challenge comes late. I know that's exactly when I should want somebody to hit it hard. My first reaction doesn't always get the memo.

prompt_id: interview.crisis-peer

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: nair.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I wouldn't take Plans just because I'm good at spotting patterns.

### beat: nair.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Campaign policy, alliances, sequencing—that's a different profession. I'd rather be a good adviser to the planner than pretend I'm one.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: nair.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  List the explanations that still fit. Then ask what move is sensible across most of them.

### beat: nair.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  We don't need the whole story before we protect ourselves from the part that could hurt us.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: nair.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I wouldn't let the most dramatic explanation push us into the most dramatic action.

### beat: nair.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we're taking a real hit next month, the move should still make sense even if our favourite explanation turns out to be wrong.
