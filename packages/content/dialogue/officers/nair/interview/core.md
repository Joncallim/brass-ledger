# Priya Nair — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: nair.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We ask for one answer too early.

### beat: nair.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Sometimes three odd facts are telling us something important and sometimes they are just three odd facts. The mistake is deciding which before we have tested more than one explanation.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: nair.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once pushed a pattern that looked like preparation for a major move.

### beat: nair.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  The unusual activity was real, but my reason for it was wrong. That was a useful failure. I had found the pattern and then become too interested in my own story about it.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: nair.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If the only reason for an irreversible action is one explanation that we cannot yet separate from another plausible one, I will tell you to slow down.

### beat: nair.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  If the action is safe under both explanations, that is different.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: nair.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I get defensive when somebody challenges a hypothesis late, after I have spent weeks building it.

### beat: nair.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  I know that is exactly when I should want a hard challenge. Knowing it does not stop the first reaction.

## sequence: crisis-peer

prompt_id: interview.crisis-peer
prompt: "Who here would you want beside you in a crisis, and why?"

### beat: nair.interview.crisis-peer.peer-reference.01
function: peer-reference

### beat: nair.interview.crisis-peer.reason.01
function: reason

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: nair.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I would not take Plans just because I am good at seeing patterns.

### beat: nair.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Building policy, alliances and a campaign sequence is a different job. I would rather be useful to the planner than pretend that makes me one.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: nair.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  List the explanations that still fit and ask what action is safe across most of them.

### beat: nair.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  We do not need the whole story before we protect ourselves from the dangerous part.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: nair.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Do not let the most dramatic explanation force the most dramatic action.

### beat: nair.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we accept a real cost next month, it should be because the action works across the uncertainty, not because one story feels compelling.
