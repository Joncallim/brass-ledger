# Nadia Yusuf — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We use 'balance' when we don't want to choose.

### beat: yusuf.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Two things can't both be first. If we lose one when the pressure comes on, say which one.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: yusuf.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once pushed a commander to choose between two priorities I thought couldn't both survive.

### beat: yusuf.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  He refused to choose. A week later, new information gave us a third option. He was right. I was trying to force clarity before it was useful.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If an order says two incompatible things are both the main effort, I'll tell you we haven't actually made the decision.

### beat: yusuf.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  Someone below us will make the choice anyway. I'd rather we own it.

prompt_id: interview.self-critique

### beat: yusuf.interview.self-critique.reason.01

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Does waiting buy us anything? If not, decide.

### beat: yusuf.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If it does, then forcing a choice now is just impatience.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Say the trade plainly: gain now, weaker next month.

### beat: yusuf.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we take the gain, fine. Own the weakness too. Don't hide it under a page of careful wording.
