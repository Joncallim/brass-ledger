# Nadia Yusuf — Core Interview Dialogue

## sequence: headquarters-failure

prompt_id: interview.headquarters-failure
prompt: "What does this headquarters get wrong most often?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.headquarters-failure.position.01
function: position
text: |
  We hide choices inside 'balance.'

### beat: yusuf.interview.headquarters-failure.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Two things cannot both be first. If we cannot have both, say which one loses.

## sequence: commander-disagreement

prompt_id: interview.commander-disagreement
prompt: "Tell me about a time you disagreed with a commander."

### beat: yusuf.interview.commander-disagreement.example.01
function: example
text: |
  I once forced a commander to choose between two priorities I thought could not both be protected.

### beat: yusuf.interview.commander-disagreement.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  He refused and held the tension for another week. He was right. New information opened a third option. Clarity is useful; premature clarity is still premature.

## sequence: red-line

prompt_id: interview.red-line
prompt: "What would make you tell me not to execute an order?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.red-line.position.01
function: position
text: |
  If an order pretends two incompatible priorities are both the main effort, I will tell you the order is avoiding the decision.

### beat: yusuf.interview.red-line.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  People below us will make the choice anyway; they will just make it without guidance.

## sequence: self-critique

prompt_id: interview.self-critique
prompt: "What are you bad at?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.self-critique.position.01
function: position
text: |
  I close rooms too early.

### beat: yusuf.interview.self-critique.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  Sometimes I have found the real choice. Sometimes I have killed a useful option before it was ready.

## sequence: crisis-peer

prompt_id: interview.crisis-peer
prompt: "Who here would you want beside you in a crisis, and why?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.crisis-peer.peer-reference.01
function: peer-reference
text: |
  Claire Dubois, despite the fact that we irritate each other.

### beat: yusuf.interview.crisis-peer.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  She sees the partner cost of forcing a choice too early. I see the cost of carrying a negotiation too long. In a crisis, I want somebody beside me who will argue the part I am most likely to rush.

## sequence: cross-post

prompt_id: interview.cross-post
prompt: "I'm considering you for another post. What worries you about it?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.cross-post.position.01
function: position
text: |
  No. I would not take Personnel just because I am willing to make hard choices about people.

### beat: yusuf.interview.cross-post.qualification.01
function: qualification
text: |
  That is not a qualification. I am more useful to Personnel as a demanding customer than as its chief.

## sequence: act-or-wait

prompt_id: interview.act-or-wait
prompt: "The picture is unclear, but time is short. Do we act or wait?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.act-or-wait.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Will waiting buy us something? If not, decide.

### beat: yusuf.interview.act-or-wait.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If it will, forcing a choice now is vanity.

## sequence: future-cost

prompt_id: interview.future-cost
prompt: "We can gain something now, but it will leave us weaker next month. What matters?"

### beat: yusuf.interview.future-cost.position.01
function: position
text: |
  Put the trade on the table in one sentence: gain now, weakness next month.

### beat: yusuf.interview.future-cost.reason.01
function: reason
text: |
  If we choose the gain, own the weakness. Do not bury it in five lines of balanced language.
