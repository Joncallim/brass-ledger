# Faction Balance

Backlink: [[world-overview]]

## Doctrine Translation Rule

Faction advantages must express CELERY's tactical patterns as tradeoffs. No faction gets a free bonus. Initiative creates burn. Mass creates neglected lanes. Deception creates self-deception risk. Security slows tempo. Mobilization creates political and economic burden. Interoperability creates coalition veto points.

## Counterplay Rules

### Against Meridian Covenant

Attack coalition timing, not just forces. Force the Covenant to choose between public assurance and quiet readiness. Deception should target alliance assumptions and parliamentary timelines.

### Against Veyr Directorate

Attack the reliability of system-level confidence. Create contradictory indicators that make centralized command choose between tempo and verification. Sustainment and civil fatigue should punish prolonged high readiness.

### Against Helix Compact

Attack legitimacy and jurisdiction. Helix has excellent logistics visibility but weak sacrifice narratives. Public hearings, contract leaks, and labor unrest should be as dangerous as kinetic losses.

### Against Sanctum League

Avoid direct attacks into prepared local defense. Pressure fuel, medical evacuation, and partner access. Force Sanctum to decide whether every inlet is sacred or some positions must be traded for endurance.

### Against Free River Mandates

Deny narrative oxygen and protect boring infrastructure. The Mandates thrive when opponents overreact. They struggle when security, repair, and local political concessions arrive together.

### Against Gray Signal International

Make attribution less important than resilience. Gray Signal wins when clients need perfect certainty. They lose when systems can absorb uncertainty without granting them bargaining power.

## Implementation Hooks

Future scenario data can encode these as doctrine profiles:

- Meridian Covenant: high alliance alignment gains, higher committee and liaison burden.
- Veyr Directorate: high mobilization and posture gains, higher deception self-capture and recovery debt.
- Helix Compact: strong constraint manipulation, low public legitimacy and fragile personnel endurance.
- Sanctum League: strong defensive incident control, limited deployable-unit ceiling and lift dependence.
- Free River Mandates: event-generation pressure and sabotage, weak sustained operations and low stockpile depth.
- Gray Signal International: deception and cyber events, poor trust recovery and high backlash when exposed.
